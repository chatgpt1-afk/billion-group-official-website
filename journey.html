Where It All Began

In 1985, the visionary brand Billion was founded, marking the beginning of a transformative journey. The first Billion Shopping Centre opened on Jalan Besar, Kajang, Selangor. This milestone laid the groundwork for countless breakthroughs and is a cornerstone in the company's enduring legacy of excellence.

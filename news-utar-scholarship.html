EDUCATION · PARTNERSHIPS / 13 APRIL 2026

UTAR and Billion Shopping Centre (Semenyih) Sdn Bhd launch scholarship collaboration to expand opportunity and strengthen industry-ready talent

The Billion Scholarship Programme connects educational opportunity with practical industry experience.

UTAR took a meaningful step in advancing student opportunity and industry-integrated education through the signing of a memorandum of agreement (MoA) with Billion Shopping Centre (Semenyih) Sdn Bhd on 13 April 2026 at UTAR Sungai Long Campus. Centred on the Billion Scholarship Programme, the collaboration reflects a shared commitment to supporting high-potential students, strengthening graduate readiness, and creating stronger connections between higher education and industry.

The partnership goes beyond financial assistance. By combining tuition fee support with structured internship opportunities, the programme is designed to help students pursue their studies with greater confidence while gaining the practical exposure needed to thrive in a competitive professional environment. It also reinforces UTAR’s commitment to producing graduates who are academically strong, professionally prepared and capable of contributing meaningfully to industry and society.

Under the agreement, Billion Shopping Centre (Semenyih) Sdn Bhd will sponsor a total of 16 students over a three-year period, comprising eight students entering or in Year 2 and eight students entering or in Year 3. Each selected student will receive up to RM16,000 per annum in tuition fee support, subject to the programme’s terms and conditions. The scholarship is open to Malaysian full-time undergraduate students from selected programmes, including retail management, business administration, logistics and supply chain management, marketing, accounting, communication, graphic design and multimedia, as well as occupational safety and health with environmental health. Priority will be given to students who demonstrate both academic merit and genuine financial need.

A key feature of the programme is the inclusion of a mandatory internship placement of at least three months with Billion Shopping Centre (Semenyih) Sdn Bhd. This component will allow sponsored students to apply classroom knowledge in a real business setting, gain industry insights and develop workplace competencies that are increasingly valued by employers. The integration of sponsorship and experiential learning reflects a more holistic approach to talent development, one that supports not only access to education but also long-term employability.

In his speech, UTAR President Ir Prof Dato’ Dr Ewe Hong Tat described the occasion as an important milestone in the shared commitment of both parties to education, opportunity and student empowerment. He noted that while education has the power to transform lives and uplift communities, many capable students still face financial barriers in pursuing higher education. He added that partnerships such as this are vital in ensuring that deserving students are given the support they need to succeed, while also broadening their learning journey through practical industry exposure.

The ceremony brought together key representatives from both UTAR and Billion Shopping Centre (Semenyih) Sdn Bhd, reflecting the strategic importance of the collaboration. Representing Billion were Chairman of Billion Group Mr Lee Yung Sen, Financial cum IT Administrator Mr Albert Chuah, Operation Director of Billion Group Mr Tan He Khon, Managing Director of Billion Group Mr Wong Chee Leong, Sales and Marketing Director of Billion Group Mr Leong Teik Ee, Group Executive Director of Billion Group Mr Lee Siew Hung, Employer Branding and Marketing Manager Mr Koo Siong Ann, and Employer Branding and Marketing Executive Ms Sofea.

Representing UTAR were its President Ir Prof Dato’ Dr Ewe Hong Tat, Dean of the Faculty of Accountancy and Management Assoc Prof Dr Pok Wei Fong, Deputy Dean of the Faculty of Creative Industries Assoc Prof Thinavan Periyayya, Head of the Department of Multimedia Design and Animation Dr Wan Irma Sabrina binti Idris, and Head of the Department of Scholarships and Financial Aid Ms Tan Wear Sim. Their presence underscored the collective resolve of both organisations to build an impactful collaboration that will benefit students while deepening engagement between academia and industry.

The Billion Scholarship Programme is expected to deliver tangible value on multiple fronts. For students, it offers meaningful financial support, structured workplace learning and stronger career pathways. For industry, it creates opportunities to engage with emerging talent early and contribute to the development of a capable graduate pipeline. For UTAR, the collaboration strengthens its strategic focus on purposeful partnerships that enhance student outcomes, elevate industry relevance and reinforce the University’s position as an institution committed to quality, innovation and future-ready education.

As UTAR continues to expand collaborations that create measurable educational and societal impact, the MoA with Billion Shopping Centre (Semenyih) Sdn Bhd stands as a strong example of how shared vision can be translated into meaningful opportunity. By aligning sponsorship with experiential learning, the initiative will widen access for deserving students while supporting the development of well-rounded graduates equipped for the demands of a dynamic economy.

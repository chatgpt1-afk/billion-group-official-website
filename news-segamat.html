Pasaraya Billion Segamat to Cease Operations on 15 November 2026

A notice dated 4 September outlines the closure and clearance schedule for Pasaraya Billion Segamat.

12 September 2026 – Pasaraya Billion, located along Jalan Ros in Segamat, is reported to be ceasing operations on 15 November 2026, according to information shared in a notice dated 4 September 2026.

The decision follows Billion’s decision not to renew the lease agreement with the property owner. In line with the closure arrangements, all tenants and resellers operating within the premises have been requested to vacate their respective spaces according to the designated schedule.

The notice outlines several phases leading up to the complete handover of the premises, covering the clearance sale period, the removal of tenants’ belongings and stock, and the final clearance of the remaining spaces.

Closure and Clearance Schedule

1 October – 15 November 2026: Clearance Sale

A clearance sale will take place during this period, running until the store’s final day of operation on 15 November 2026. All sales activities will cease once operations officially end on that date.

16 – 23 November 2026: Vacating and Relocation

Following the cessation of business operations, tenants will be required to vacate their respective spaces and remove their equipment, fixtures and stock from the premises.

24 November – 10 December 2026: Final Vacating Phase

The final phase will involve completing the remaining clearance work to ensure that all spaces are vacated and the premises are returned to the property owner by 10 December 2026 at the latest.

A Longstanding Presence in Segamat

Pasaraya Billion Segamat has long been part of the local business landscape, serving as one of the commercial premises familiar to the Segamat community.

The reported closure marks a change for the premises, which has been part of the area’s business environment for many years. News of the upcoming cessation of operations has prompted reactions among members of the local community, with residents reflecting on their experiences and memories associated with Billion Segamat.

As the scheduled closure date approaches, the premises will enter its final period of sales before the subsequent vacating and handover process takes place.

Billion Segamat’s longstanding presence has made it a familiar name within the local community, and its upcoming closure invites residents to look back on the memories and experiences they have shared at the premises.

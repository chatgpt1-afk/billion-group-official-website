Billion Group Expands into Malacca Market Through Strategic Partnerships

Billion Group marks its entry into Malacca with strategic partnerships for Klebang Cove Residensi and Taman Sutera Wangi.

11 April 2026 – Billion Group is proud to announce the successful Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) signing ceremony, marking a significant milestone in the Group’s strategic expansion into the Malacca market.

Held on 11 April 2026, the MOU signing ceremony formalised new partnerships for two landmark projects: Klebang Cove Residensi and Taman Sutera Wangi. This development represents an important step in establishing Billion Group’s presence in Malacca and extending its retail offerings to a new community.

With a heritage of 40 years built on trust and value, Billion Group continues to pursue opportunities to serve regional and growing markets across the nation. The Group’s entry into Malacca reflects its ongoing commitment to bringing convenient retail solutions closer to the communities it serves.

Through these new partnerships, Billion Group aims to establish a convenient, one-stop retail destination for residents of Klebang Cove Residensi and Taman Sutera Wangi. By integrating grocery and lifestyle offerings, the Group seeks to support the everyday needs of local families and provide a convenient shopping destination right at their doorstep.

Key Highlights

Billion’s First Malacca Outlet

The expansion marks Billion Group’s first outlet in Malacca, establishing the brand’s presence in a new market and opening the way to connect with the local community.

Community-Centric Retail Experience

Through its planned retail presence serving Klebang Cove Residensi and Taman Sutera Wangi, Billion Group aims to bring grocery and lifestyle offerings closer to residents, supporting their daily needs through a convenient one-stop retail destination.

Strategic Expansion and Nationwide Growth

This milestone reflects Billion Group’s continued efforts to expand into regional and growing markets nationwide, building on its 40-year heritage of trust and value while reaching new communities.

Billion Group extends its sincere appreciation to its partners for their trust in the Billion brand and for making this milestone possible. The Group looks forward to building strong partnerships, connecting with the Malacca community and empowering its new brand partners in the region.

As Billion Group continues its expansion journey, this milestone represents another step forward in its mission to serve communities through convenient retail offerings and trusted value.

WHAT WE DO

Everyday Essentials, Endless Possibilities

We have always strived to offer a wide range of retail experiences, and today, with 56 outlets (and counting!), we continue to thrive in multiple business categories, including hypermarkets, supermarkets, and convenience stores.

Behind our success lies a powerful partnership with top suppliers, bringing the best fresh produce, seafood, and a variety of goods from Fresh Market, Seafood, Supermarket, Softline and Hardline categories to our valued customers.

However, we are more than just the products we offer. The history and evolution of our brands, Billion and Pantai Timor, have unique stories that shaped our success and vision. What makes us different? Why do we stand out in the market?

OUR 40-YEAR MILESTONE

Celebrating 40 Years of Excellence

What started as a humble shopping centre in Kajang soon grew into something much larger. Over four decades, we have expanded across Peninsular Malaysia, opening new locations to bring our trusted shopping experience to communities far and wide.

Our network includes not only our original shopping centre but also hypermarkets and convenience stores that cater to the diverse needs of our customers.

As we marked our 40th anniversary, we reflected on the journey with pride. From our beginnings in Kajang to our growth across the country, we’ve remained dedicated to offering exceptional value and convenience to everyone who walks through our doors.

The success we’ve achieved over the years is a testament to our strong relationships with our customers, partners, and retail community.

Thank you for being part of our journey. Here’s to 40 years of milestones and many more years of success as we continue to grow and serve customers across Peninsular Malaysia.

Billion

Our proud homegrown Malaysian retail heritage.

We are a Proud Homegrown Malaysian Retail Brand

As a leading homegrown retail company in Malaysia, we take immense pride in our rich heritage and strong foundations that have guided our growth over the years. Established in 1985, we began our journey with a simple yet powerful vision — to provide high-quality products and exceptional customer service to the communities we serve. Over the years, this vision has evolved into a commitment to become a key player in Malaysia's retail industry.

From our humble beginnings, we have steadily expanded our presence across Peninsular Malaysia, earning a reputation for reliability and value. With a strong foothold in key regions such as the Central, Southern, East Coast, Perak, and Northern areas, we are proud to have established ourselves as a trusted name in retail, serving millions of customers and touching every corner of the nation.

Our diverse portfolio, which includes everything from hypermarkets and supermarkets to convenience stores, reflects our ability to adapt to changing market demands while staying true to our commitment to quality. Our strategic positioning in these regions has allowed us to effectively cater to the varied needs of customers across different communities, ensuring that we are always within reach, offering products and services that make a meaningful impact in everyday lives.

Through decades of growth, innovation, and a customer-centric approach, we have not only built a business but a lasting legacy that continues to thrive and evolve. Our ability to adapt to regional preferences, coupled with a nationwide network of outlets, positions us as a cornerstone in the retail sector, always striving to provide the best shopping experience for all Malaysians.

With our slogan “Jimat & Menyakinkan Anda”, we are committed to providing high-quality products at competitive prices, ensuring that every customer experience is not only satisfying but memorable.

Pantai Timor

Rooted in the East Coast, connected to the community.

We are a Proud Homegrown Malaysian Retail Brand

On the East Coast of Malaysia, we proudly operate under the Pantai Timor brand, a decision rooted in our commitment to better engage with the local community and meet their specific needs. By using the Pantai Timor name, we are able to tailor our services and offerings to resonate with the distinct preferences of this region, fostering a stronger sense of local connection and trust.

Whether through our commitment to competitive pricing, quality goods, or exceptional customer service, the Pantai Timor brand represents our dedication to delivering an outstanding shopping experience, grounded in the values of trust, reliability, and community focus — values that have been the cornerstone of the Billion Group since its inception.

This regional identity allows us to be more attuned to the cultural and economic landscape of the East Coast, ensuring that our customers feel closer to the brand and confident in their shopping experience. At the same time, we remain a proud part of the Billion Group, continuously upholding the same high standards of service, product quality, and value that our name is renowned for across Malaysia.

With our slogan “Jimat & Menyakinkan Anda”, we are committed to providing high-quality products at competitive prices, ensuring that every customer experience is not only satisfying but memorable.

In 1985, the visionary brand Billion was founded, marking the beginning of a transformative journey. The first Billion Shopping Centre opened on Jalan Besar, Kajang, Selangor.
